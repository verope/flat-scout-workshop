## What this measures
Whether the flat is big enough to live in with a table people can sit around
and a dog bed that is not in a doorway. Fifty square metres is the floor rather
than the target.

## Rubric
Graded by code, in square metres. The EPC certificate's stated floor area is
preferred; the listing's own square footage, converted, is used where there is
no certificate.

**10** — 60 m² or more.
**8** — 50 m² up to 60 m².
**0** — under 50 m².

## Evidence, and what not to read into it
Where the listing's square footage and the certificate's floor area disagree by
more than about a tenth, the size is unresolved rather than one of them being
right — an agent can attach the certificate of a different unit — so the
Criterion comes back unknown and raises the contradiction as a concern. Nothing
is estimated from a floorplan's room dimensions or from the number of rooms.
Unknown when neither source states an area.
