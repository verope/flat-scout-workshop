## What this measures
Whether the living areas — the reception, the hallway, the kitchen — have
floors that wipe clean.

## Rubric
**10** — wood, engineered wood, laminate, tile or vinyl stated through the
living areas.
**6** — hard floors stated in the reception or the kitchen, with carpet stated
in the bedrooms only.
**0** — carpet stated through the living areas.

## Evidence, and what not to read into it
Adverts almost never mention flooring, so this Criterion is usually unknown,
and unknown is the correct answer. Read it off the description and the key
features only. The listing's photographs are not available to you: do not infer
flooring from the building's age, from a room's name, from "newly refurbished"
or from anything but a stated material. Return null otherwise.
