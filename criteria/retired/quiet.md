## What this measures
Whether the couple will hear the street. This is deliberately separate from
warmth: an EPC band is evidence about heat and says nothing about acoustics, so
a flat can be cheap to heat and still be loud.

## Rubric
**10** — a modern or build-to-rent scheme with acoustic glazing stated, or a
building set back from any through road.
**8** — modern construction, double or triple glazed, on a residential street.
**5** — the advert claims a quiet or residential location, with nothing said
about the glazing or the construction.
**2** — on a main road, above a pub or a restaurant, or beside a railway, with
nothing said about glazing.
**0** — the advert itself concedes noise, or it is a period conversion with
single glazing on a main road.

## Evidence, and what not to read into it
Build-to-rent is welcome and not a compromise. A charming Victorian conversion
with single glazing is a no. Do not read a band, a price or a postcode as
evidence about noise. Every grade above needs the advert to have said
something: return null if it says nothing about the street, the glazing or
the construction. Silence is not a 5.
